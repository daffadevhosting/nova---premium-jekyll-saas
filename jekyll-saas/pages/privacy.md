At **NOVA**, we take your privacy and server-cluster data security extremely seriously. This Privacy Policy documents how we process and secure any information transmitted through our systems.

### 1. Data Collection
We only collect active workflow execution logs, payload statistics, and basic account profile details (such as your name and business email) necessary to run our service. We **never** inspect, store, or sell any of your secure data payloads or API tokens.

### 2. Encryption standards
All data transmitted through our APIs is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3 pipelines. At rest, all workspace database records are fully encrypted using military-grade AES-256 protocols.

### 3. Cookies
We utilize minimal cookies to manage your active dashboard sessions and remember your light/dark mode UI preferences. We do not run intrusive cross-site trackers or sell lists to third parties.

For questions, contact privacy@nova-saas.com.
