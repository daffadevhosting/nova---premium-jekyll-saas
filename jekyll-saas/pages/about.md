Welcome to **NOVA**. We are a distributed team of engineers, designers, and systems architects dedicated to building the future of automated workflow orchestration.

### Our Mission

Our primary objective is simple: **Build smarter. Ship faster.** We believe that development and product teams spend too much time building glue code, configuring custom servers, and mapping API endpoints. We wanted to design a sleek, lightweight, yet military-grade system where pipelines run fully on autopilot.

### The Team

NOVA was started in 2026 by a group of developers who grew tired of standard, bloated enterprise orchestration software. Today, over 10,000+ teams around the world rely on NOVA to maintain their active service clusters and run testing suites.

We are fully self-funded, profitable, and remote-first. Feel free to reach out or explore our open opportunities on our careers page!
